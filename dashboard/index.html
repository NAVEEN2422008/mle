About the Solar Flare Early Warning System

What is this project?
The Solar Flare Early Warning System (SFEWS) is a deep-learning space weather operations cockpit designed to forecast dangerous M- and X-class solar flares 15 to 60 minutes before peak destructive radiation reaches Earth.

Why is it important?
Solar superflares emit high-energy X-rays and Coronal Mass Ejections (CMEs) that disrupt GNSS satellite navigation, cause HF radio blackouts for aviation, and induce catastrophic geomagnetic currents in power grids. Early warning allows satellite operators and grid managers to execute defensive safe-modes.

What datasets and missions power it?
- ISRO Aditya-L1 (XSM / SoLEXS): Hard X-ray spectrometer in halo orbit around Sun-Earth Lagrange Point 1 (PRADAN Level-1 data).
- NOAA GOES-18: Continuous Soft X-Ray irradiance (0.1–0.8 nm).
- NASA SDO / HMI: Line-of-sight magnetograms of active region magnetic shear.

AI Model Architecture:
A 4-tier ensemble featuring a 30-Dimensional Spatio-Temporal Graph Transformer and CNN-LSTM, constrained by Physics-Informed Neural Network (PINN) magneto-hydrodynamic (MHD) thermal conduction loss.
