Physics 1: The Neupert Effect

When magnetic reconnection accelerates electrons downward, they collide with the dense chromosphere, emitting non-thermal hard X-rays (HXR) via bremsstrahlung. This suddenly heats plasma, which evaporates upward to fill coronal loops with hot thermal soft X-rays (SXR):

d(SXR)/dt = α · HXR(t) - β · SXR(t)

Because heating accumulates over time, the soft X-ray curve mirrors the time integral of the hard X-ray pulse.
